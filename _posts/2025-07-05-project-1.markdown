description: I am designing a RISC-V 32E microcontroller unit (MCU) aimed at sensor modules for smart home and smart city AI home servers. The project involves developing a comprehensive RTL implementation in Verilog, focusing on power-efficient design and real-time sensor interfacing. I architected and implemented the complete micro-architecture of the RV32E CPU core and its peripherals, including an efficient power management system, SRAM controller, SPI interface, and external device connectivity solutions for ASIC integration. At the gate level, I implemented and integrated key modules such as the power management unit, clock control system, on-chip RAM, I/O ports, and advanced debugging mechanisms, all optimized for performance, power, and area. The design targets low-power operation using TSMC’s 65nm process and achieves synthesis and timing closure through the full ASIC flow. For the implementation, I utilized Cadence Virtuoso for analog integration, Synopsys IC Compiler II for floorplanning and place-and-route, and Synopsys Design Compiler for logic synthesis and timing optimization.
