About Me

Hi, I am a passionate engineer with a dream of providing smart home services to support all people. I hope the system I am dreaming of will support everyone in the world so that their lives become more efficient and well-organized.

Welcome to a brief introduction about myself. I am a dedicated ASIC/embedded systems engineer with a vision for a smarter, interconnected future.

My expertise spans diverse projects, including RF circuit analysis, Verilog-based MIPS computer design, CMOS amplifier creation using TSMC chips, and building communication networks with Arduino and Raspberry Pi.

I'm continuously expanding my skill set with projects in Python, C/C++, web development with React, and cloud services like AWS IoT. My goal is to integrate hardware and software seamlessly to create innovative solutions that enhance everyday life.

When I'm not engineering, you can find me cooking and hiking. Feel free to ask me to join if you are interested!

Feel free to explore my work and reach out if you'd like to collaborate or discuss innovative ideas.
