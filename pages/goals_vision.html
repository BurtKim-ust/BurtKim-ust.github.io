Core

Guiding Principles

Excellence in Execution: I believe that great engineering is not just about having innovative ideas, but about executing them with precision and attention to detail. Every circuit, every line of code, and every design decision should reflect a commitment to quality.

Continuous Learning: I don't learn just because the field of engineering evolves rapidly. Rather, I'm committed to learning because it gives me a feeling of aliveness. I feel alive when I learn new things and overcome challenges through knowledge acquisition.

Collaboration & Mentorship: The best engineering happens in teams where knowledge flows freely. I value collaborative environments and take pride in mentoring junior engineers while learning from experienced colleagues.

Sustainable Innovation: Technology should serve humanity's short-term and long-term interests. I'm passionate about developing solutions that are not only technically excellent but also environmentally responsible and socially beneficial.
