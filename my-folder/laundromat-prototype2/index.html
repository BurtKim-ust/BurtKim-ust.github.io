These humble spaces have woven themselves into the fabric of our city, becoming gathering places where all races and backgrounds converge. In the rhythm of the washing machine—fill, agitate, rinse, spin—we find a metaphor for New York itself: diverse communities mixing, neighborhoods transforming, necessities bringing us together, and through it all, a shared identity emerging.
